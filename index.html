Technical Notes

  • F0 is estimated with a simplified YIN algorithm and optional median filtering.
  • Audio recording uses the browser MediaRecorder API; analysis remains local.
  • Playback cursor is synchronized across waveform, pitch and spectrogram.
  • Audio is processed locally in the browser.
  • For formal formant analysis, validate with Praat/LPC.
